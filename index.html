You were right to keep asking why.

The Why Loop makes short math videos. Each one starts with a question from everyday life and ends when the answer makes sense.

The videos are posted on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. To publish them, we use a small uploader that runs on our own computer and posts only to our own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

The Why Loop uploader and YouTube

The uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload our videos to our own YouTube channel. Before using it, read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
