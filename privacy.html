Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Who uses the uploader

The Why Loop uploader is used only by The Why Loop to publish its own videos to its own accounts. It has no other users, and it does not collect data about viewers or other people.

What it accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information of our own account, and permission to send our videos to that account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to our own channel and to read their processing status.
  • Instagram and Facebook: permission to publish Reels to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page.

Where data is kept

The access tokens the platforms issue are stored only on our own computer. They are not sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them. We keep a log of what was posted and when, with the post IDs the platforms return.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting access

Access can be removed at any time in the security or connected apps settings of each platform. Tokens are also deleted from our computer when we stop using the uploader.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/TheWhyLoop/TheWhyLoop.
